# Final - Job Interview

**Discipline:** Software Engineering
**Job title:** Junior QA and DevOps Engineer
**Difficulty:** 1 / 5
**Modes:** Practice, Final

## Job Description

We are looking for a Junior QA and DevOps Engineer to join our growing Software Engineering team. In this hybrid role, you will play a critical part in ensuring the reliability and stability of our applications while streamlining our deployment processes. You will work closely with developers to automate testing suites, maintain our CI/CD pipelines, and monitor system performance, helping us deliver high-quality software through a culture of continuous improvement and automation.

## Responsibilities
* Develop and maintain automated test scripts for web and API-based applications to ensure high code coverage and functional reliability.
* Assist in the management and optimization of CI/CD pipelines to facilitate frequent and stable software releases.
* Perform manual regression and smoke testing during release cycles to identify bugs and usability issues before they reach production.
* Monitor application logs and system health metrics to proactively identify and report potential performance bottlenecks or service interruptions.
* Document technical processes, deployment procedures, and test cases to maintain a comprehensive internal knowledge base.
* Collaborate with senior engineers to troubleshoot environment-specific issues across development, staging, and production environments.

## Requirements
* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
* Fundamental understanding of at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, JavaScript, or Bash.
* Familiarity with the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and basic quality assurance methodologies.
* Basic knowledge of Linux environments and containerization concepts, such as Docker.
* Strong analytical problem-solving skills and a high level of attention to detail when documenting software defects.

## Learning Outcomes

- Automation - Design and implement automated testing and delivery workflows, valuing disciplined practices that foster trust, quality, and continuous improvement.
- Observability - Analyze software systems using evidence and metrics, cultivating intellectual humility and informed judgment.
- Collaboration - Collaborate effectively with peers to improve software quality and delivery, developing habits of respect, accountability, and Christlike service.
- Leadership - Evaluate the practical and ethical implications of QA and DevOps decisions, strengthening integrity and responsible stewardship of technology and its impact on others.
- Learning - Assess emerging tools and practices in quality assurance and DevOps, fostering lifelong learning, curiosity, and creativity while magnifying the ability to serve and bless others.
